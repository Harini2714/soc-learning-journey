# Man-in-the-Middle Traffic Sniffing Notes

## What is a Man-in-the-Middle Attack?

A Man-in-the-Middle attack happens when an attacker positions themselves between two communicating systems and observes or manipulates the traffic.

## Ettercap

Ettercap is a tool used to study Man-in-the-Middle concepts and traffic sniffing in a lab environment.

## Traffic Sniffing

Traffic sniffing is the process of observing network traffic.

In insecure environments, sensitive information may be exposed if proper encryption is not used.

## Security Importance

This concept helps understand the importance of:

- HTTPS
- Encryption
- Secure authentication
- Network monitoring
- Detection of suspicious traffic

## Learning Outcome

Understood how traffic interception works at a basic level and why secure communication is important in cybersecurity.
